Brand names

20 candidates, 4 themes.

All short enough to own. All evocative for invoice finance specifically. Domain availability is heuristic — verify before committing.

Theme 01 · Action verbs

"This is what we do for you"

Direct, plain English. Pair best with the Friendly brand.

Theme 02 · Latin & abstract

"We're a serious fintech"

Made-up or Latin roots. Pair best with the Fintech brand.

Theme 03 · Direct + evocative

"Honest, descriptive, owned"

Single common words. Pair best with the Editorial brand.

Theme 04 · Coined / portmanteau

"We're a category of one"

Words you have to learn — but you own them completely.

A pragmatic note on domains: all single English words (Float, Snap, Pulse, Tide, Ledger) are almost certainly taken as .com. For UK invoice finance specifically, .finance is a legitimate TLD that signals trust without paying $5,000+ for the .com. .co and .ai are increasingly acceptable for fintech. Verify with a quick check on Namecheap before falling in love.
Top 5

My five favourites — in their natural brand.

Each name shown in the brand direction it pairs best with, so you can see what you'd actually be living with.

FRIENDLY · #1 PICK
Float.
cash from invoices, finally.
Why it works: existing finance term so it carries credibility instantly, but light and human feeling. Verbs cleanly ("Float your invoices"). Memorable. Domain: float.finance or float.co.
FINTECH · #2 PICK
Nimbo
your invoices, paid instantly.
Why it works: sounds like "nimble" without being literal. Modern, AI-friendly. Two-syllable, owned, abstract. Pairs naturally with .ai domain. nimbo.ai.
EDITORIAL · #3 PICK
Ledger.
Invoice finance, properly.
Why it works: the literal name of the asset we're financing. Confident, clear, owned. Used by Ledger crypto-hardware, but in finance the term is universal — own the SaaS angle. ledger.finance.
EDITORIAL · #4 PICK
Pulse.
The rhythm of getting paid.
Why it works: rhythm of cash hitting your account. Active, healthy, vital. Has nice secondary meaning around heart-rate / business health. pulse.finance.
FRIENDLY · #5 PICK
Hatch.
unlock what your invoices are sitting on.
Why it works: evokes opening up locked value. Short, memorable, owned. Already used by some startups (verify) but for invoice finance it's a clean fit. hatch.finance.
